Brisbane Street Food Festival

Discover flavours from around the world at Brisbane's ultimate street food celebration. Enjoy local vendors, international cuisine, live entertainment and an unforgettable evening by the river.

Date 12 September 2026
Time 4:00 PM - 10:00 PM
Venue South Bank Parklands, Brisbane
Tickets From $25 per person

About this event

Brisbane Street Food Festival brings together some of the city's favourite food trucks, independent vendors and local chefs for an evening dedicated to great food and community. Visitors can explore dishes inspired by cuisines from across Asia, Europe, the Middle East and South America.

Alongside the food stalls, the festival features live music, outdoor seating and cooking demonstrations throughout the evening. Whether you're coming with friends, family or simply looking to discover something new, there will be plenty to taste and explore.

What to expect

30+ Food Vendors Local favourites and international flavours.
Live Entertainment Music and performances throughout the evening.
Dietary Options Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options available.
All Ages A family-friendly food festival.
